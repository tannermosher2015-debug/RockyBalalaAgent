Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do you work with both buyers and sellers?
Yes — both. I represent buyers looking for the right home on Maui and sellers who want honest pricing and real marketing. If you're doing both at once, I'll help you line up the timing so you're not stuck between two closings.
Which parts of Maui do you cover?
All of it. I'm based in Wailuku and work island-wide — Kahului, Kīhei, Wailea, Lahaina, Pukalani, Makawao, Hāna and the towns in between. Each area has its own pricing and quirks, and I'll tell you straight which fits what you're after.
I'm buying from off-island — can you help remotely?
Absolutely. A lot of my clients start their search from the mainland. I do video walkthroughs, send honest neighborhood breakdowns, and handle the on-island legwork so you're never guessing about a place you haven't stood in yet.
What's the first step to selling my Maui home?
A conversation and a real look at your home — no pressure. I'll walk it with you, give you an honest market price (not an inflated one to win the listing), and lay out exactly what's worth doing before we go live and what isn't.
How do I reach you?
Call or text me at (808) 283-1800, or email waenagroup@gmail.com. I answer my own phone — you'll get me, not a call center.
